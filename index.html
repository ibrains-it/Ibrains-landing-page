What does IBrains do? add

IBrains is an AI engineering studio. We build voice agents, industrial IoT systems, mobile apps, and EduFlex, our adaptive learning platform for schools and training providers.

What is EduFlex? add

EduFlex is IBrains' adaptive learning platform. It adjusts course material to each student's pace and performance in real time, so instructors can spend less time re-teaching.

Does IBrains build custom voice AI agents? add

Yes. We design, train, and deploy conversational voice agents for inbound and outbound support, tuned to your call flows and integrated with your existing systems.

Does IBrains work on IoT and hardware projects? add

Yes. We build edge-to-cloud IoT architecture, including device connectivity, real-time telemetry, and automation for industrial and commercial deployments.
